  - **A hook posts your progress**, an ephemeral line on each call: your plan's step in
    progress, else the phase, then what the call does. Checks, pushes, sub-agents and CI
    waits get an action, ticks and an outcome. Post no progress beyond what a brief names: every post spends Linear's
    request budget.
  - **A `response` ends a round:** Linear shows a session whose newest activity is one as
    finished, and stops its timer there, however long you then work. Post one only for
    the final summary or to answer a message; the hook's next line shows you working
    again. End every round with a `response` or an `elicitation`: Linear says "Thinking"
    while the newest activity is a thought or an action.
